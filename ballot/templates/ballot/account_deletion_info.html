ATL's Hottest Account Deletion

ATL's Hottest account holders can permanently delete their account from Account Settings after signing in.

How to delete your account

Sign in to ATL's Hottest, open Account Settings, choose Delete My Account, confirm your password, and complete the deletion confirmation.

Deleting an ATL's Hottest account removes the ATL's Hottest account and its associated account access. A separately connected MajicMall Megaverse account is not deleted by this action.
