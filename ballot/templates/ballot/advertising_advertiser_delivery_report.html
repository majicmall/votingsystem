About This Proof-of-Play Report

A purchased appearance is counted once for each advertising appearance acquired by the campaign. A verified play is counted only after the corresponding delivery reservation completes the server-side acknowledgement process and is recorded as played. Media values shown in this report use the historical campaign spend recorded at the time the advertising inventory was purchased.
